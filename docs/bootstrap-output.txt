Bootstrap study: standard errors of three estimates of the center
Population: Normal(mean 100, SD 10), with probability 0.01 of an outlier from Normal(mean 200, SD 1)
100 repetitions x 100 bootstrap resamples; trimmed mean drops 5% from each end

     n   Theory (no outliers)   Sample mean   Bootstrap mean   Bootstrap median   Bootstrap trimmed mean    Most stable
    25                   2.00          3.10             3.09               2.29                     2.57         median
   100                   1.00          1.30             1.30               1.22                     1.04   trimmed mean
   225                   0.67          0.95             0.96               0.76                     0.68   trimmed mean
   400                   0.50          0.70             0.70               0.64                     0.54   trimmed mean

Lower is more stable. Theory is SD/sqrt(n): the mean's standard error if there were no outliers.
